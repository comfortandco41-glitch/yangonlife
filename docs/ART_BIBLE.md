# Art Bible & Visual Style Guide — Yangon Frontline

## 1. Visual Direction & Style Identity
- **Style:** Stylized Low-Poly Urban Realism.
- **Tone:** Gritty yet vibrant tropical urban atmosphere. High contrast sunlight, warm dusty tones, aged colonial plaster, and saturated street accents.
- **Geometry Rules:** Flat-shaded or soft-shaded low-polygon meshes with crisp silhouette readability. No dense subdivided meshes.

---

---

## 3. Faction Silhouettes & Uniforms
1. **MILITARY Faction:**
   - **Colors:** Olive Drab (`#3A4D39`), Slate Charcoal (`#2C302E`).
   - **Gear:** Low-poly ballistic helmet, tactical goggles, plate carrier vest, cargo combat trousers, combat boots.
   - **Silhouette:** Sturdy, squared shoulders, high-coverage armor profile.
2. **PDF (People's Defence Force):**
   - **Colors:** Faded Denim / Earth Tan (`#5C6B73`, `#8D7B68`), Crimson identifier band (`#B80000`).
   - **Gear:** Tactical chest rig over civilian shirt/jacket, rolled sleeves, cloth bandana or boonie hat, sneakers/hiking boots.
   - **Silhouette:** Lean, agile, asymmetric gear distribution.

---

## 4. Environment & Building Language
- **Colonial Tenements:** 3 to 4 stories tall, repeated wooden shutter louvers, arched windows, ornate roof cornices, and exterior balconies.
- **Street-Level Shophouses:** Metal roll-up shutters, colorful canvas awnings, hand-painted signs.
- **Urban Clutter:** Low-poly roadside tea shop stools (red and blue plastic cylinders), small wooden square tables, hanging tangled electrical wires between utility poles.

---

## 5. Asset Conventions & Licensing
- **Naming Standard:**
  - Meshes: `mesh_<category>_<name>_<variant>.obj` / `.glb`
  - Materials: `mat_<name>.tres`
  - Textures: `tex_<name>_<albedo|norm|rough>.png`
- **Licensing Integrity:** All assets must be original procedural/custom low-poly geometry or MIT/CC0 licensed. No proprietary or unlicensed models may be introduced.
